Generative Photography · CVPR 2025 Highlight

Control the camera, preserve the world.

Control focal length, bokeh, shutter speed, and color temperature while preserving scene identity.

ProblemCamera prompts do not obey optics.
IdeaJointly model a scene across camera settings.
ResultControllable effects with stronger scene consistency.

Core role Camera-physics consistency and control → physical motion.

NewtonGen · ICLR 2026

Give video generation a sense of physics.

Learn Neural Newtonian Dynamics, then guide video generation with explicit physical states.

ProblemVideo models imitate motion without understanding it.
IdeaLinear neural ODEs plus a residual model.
ResultDiverse motion with explicit parameter control.

Core role Motion-physics consistency and control → complete 4D worlds.

SeeU · CVPR 2026

See beyond the observed time and space.

Lift sparse 2D observations into a continuous 4D world across unseen times and viewpoints.

ProblemObservations are sparse in both time and viewpoint.
IdeaCouple continuous dynamics with a 4D world model.
ResultTemporal, spatial, and edited unseen-world generation.

Core role Space-time consistency and control → goal-directed futures.

OptiWorld · Under Review

Plan a better future, then render it.

Optimize goal-directed trajectories under physical constraints before rendering the future.

ProblemPlausible futures can still be unsafe or inefficient.
IdeaOptimize trajectories before video synthesis.
ResultGoal-conditioned and counterfactual world generation.

Core role Unite physical consistency with explicit control over the generated future.
