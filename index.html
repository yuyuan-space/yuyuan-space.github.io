Yu Yuan

My name is Yu Yuan (袁煜), a PhD candidate at Purdue ECE, under the supervision of Prof. Stanley H. Chan. Prior to this, I obtained my master and bachelor degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2023 and 2020, respectively.

I aim to build physics-consistent and controllable generative worlds. My work sits at the intersection of generative modeling, physical understanding, and computational photography, enforcing space-time-camera consistency in generative systems.

I am seeking internship and full-time Research Scientist opportunities in Video Generation and Physical World Models, with graduation expected by May 2027. Feel free to reach out!
